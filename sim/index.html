SDM26 Driver-in-Loop

Sun Devil Motorsports at FSAE Michigan 2026

Traced 2026 Michigan geometry from the Helios lap sim, resampled to 1 m. Cones score +2 s each; leaving the course is a DOO.

Driver aids

Off by default: the real car has none of these. Turn them on to learn the course, off to set a time.

Throttle map

Accelerator pedal to throttle plate. The map is applied before traction control, the engine and the audio see "throttle".

Electronic throttle map

Live setup changes (brake bias, roll stiffness) are on the d-pad in the car and in the Vehicle model tab here.
