SDM26 Driver-in-Loop

Sun Devil Motorsports at FSAE Michigan 2026

Traced 2026 Michigan geometry from the Helios lap sim, resampled to 1 m. Cones score +2 s each; leaving the course voids the run (autocross) or the lap (endurance).

Recording

Every run is logged at 100 Hz and written where Helios can find it: lap and sector times, the whole car state, and enough of it to replay the drive. Put your name on it so the times are yours. Helios shares every run's time with the team; the lap itself is uploaded only for your best 3 and latest 3 on each course, and all of it stays here.

Run-to-run setup

What the team changes between runs. The same values as the Vehicle model sheet and the in-car setup menu; roll stiffness and brake bias also have their own buttons (Controls → Setup).

Driver aids

Off by default: the real car has none of these. Turn them on to learn the course, off to set a time.

Dash

The car carries a real AiM Strada on the column, above the wheel. If reading it at its real size does not suit you, the old screen dash is still here.

Throttle map

Accelerator pedal to throttle plate. The map is applied before traction control, the engine and the audio see "throttle".

Electronic throttle map

Live setup changes (brake bias, roll stiffness) are on the d-pad in the car and in the Vehicle model tab here.
